Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order? You receive an order confirmation by email right after checkout. For delivery status, reply to that email or write to info@selogisticsllc.com with your order number.
How much is delivery? Flat $4.99 per order — free on orders over $35.
Is my payment secure? Yes. Card payments are processed by Stripe; your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored by us.
What if my item arrives damaged or I want to return it? Contact us at info@selogisticsllc.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo — we will make it right.
