How to delete your account

To delete your Elhamix account and all data associated with it:

  1. Send an email to info.elhamix@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Elhamix account.
  3. We will confirm your identity and delete your account within 7 days.

How to delete your data without deleting your account

If you want us to delete your personal data (name, address, order history) but keep your account:

  1. Send an email to info.elhamix@gmail.com from your account email.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Elhamix data and tell us which data to remove.
  3. We will process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name and email).
  • Your saved addresses and order history.
  • Any product reviews you submitted.

Order records needed for tax and legal purposes may be kept in anonymized form as required by law.
