Shipping Policy — Elhamix

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to orders placed in the Elhamix mobile app (SE Logistics LLC, dba Elhamix). Orders placed on Amazon are shipped under Amazon's own policies.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses within the United States only (all 50 states, using a US 5-digit ZIP code). We do not currently ship internationally through the app.

Delivery charges

  • Flat delivery fee: $4.99 per order.
  • Free delivery on orders over $35 (judged on the discounted product subtotal, after any promotional code).
  • The exact delivery charge for your order is always shown at checkout before you pay.

Processing and delivery time

  • Orders are processed and handed to the carrier after payment is confirmed; the app shows your order status (Processing → On the way → Delivered).
  • Estimated delivery: 5–10 days after your order is on its way. This is an estimate, not a guarantee; carrier delays can occur.

Tracking

When your order ships, its status is updated in the app (My Orders) and, where a carrier tracking number is available, it is shown there with a link to the carrier. You also receive order updates by email.

Address accuracy

Please make sure your delivery address and ZIP code are correct at checkout — we ship to the address on the order and cannot redirect a parcel once it is on its way. If an address error causes a failed delivery, contact us at info@selogisticsllc.com and we will help resolve it.

Damaged or missing deliveries

If your order arrives damaged, or does not arrive within the estimated window, contact us within 14 days of delivery (or of the expected delivery date) with your order number — see our Returns & Refunds Policy. We will make it right.
