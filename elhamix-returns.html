Returns & Refunds Policy — Elhamix

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to orders placed in the Elhamix mobile app (SE Logistics LLC, dba Elhamix). Orders placed on Amazon are returned under Amazon's own policies.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items — always covered

If your item arrives damaged or defective, or we sent the wrong item, contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo — we will make it right with a replacement or a full refund, including the delivery fee.

Changed your mind

Contact us within 14 days of delivery to request a return of an unused item in its original condition. Once we approve the request and receive the item back, we refund the product price to your original payment method. (The 14-day window matches the commitment already published on our Support page.)

How to start a return

  • In the app: open My Orders, choose the order, and submit a return request — or
  • By email: write to info@selogisticsllc.com or info.elhamix@gmail.com with your order number and the reason.

We reply with the return instructions and the address to send the item to. Please do not send items back before your request is approved.

Refunds

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe.
  • Approved refunds for damaged/defective/wrong items are processed as soon as the request is approved; change-of-mind refunds are processed after we receive and check the returned item.
  • After we issue a refund, your bank may take several business days to show it on your statement.
  • Your order status in the app is updated to Refunded once the refund is complete.

Cancellations

If your order has not shipped yet, contact us as soon as possible at info@selogisticsllc.com and we will cancel it and refund you in full.
